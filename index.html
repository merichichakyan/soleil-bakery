Our Story

A dream baked in
Paris, warmed in Yerevan

Founded in 2026, Soleil Bakery was born from a deep love for the art of French patisserie, blended seamlessly with the warmth of Armenian hospitality.

It is the dream-turned-reality of master baker Ann, who spent years perfecting her craft alongside Paris's finest pastry masters — bringing those golden mornings back home.

What Makes Us Special

We believe that true quality lies in the details. That is why we bake in small batches throughout the day, using only pristine local organic ingredients and exquisite butter sourced from select artisans. Every bite is a little piece of morning sunshine.
