Our Story

Where French craft meets Armenian warmth.

"Every bite is a little piece of morning sunshine."

Founded in 2026, Soleil Bakery was born from a deep love for the art of French patisserie, blended seamlessly with the warmth of Armenian hospitality.

It is the dream-turned-reality of master baker Ann, who spent years perfecting her craft alongside Paris's finest pastry masters.

We believe that true quality lies in the details. That is why we bake in small batches throughout the day, using only pristine local organic ingredients and exquisite butter sourced from select artisans.
