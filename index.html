Our Story

Crafted with love, baked with tradition

Founded in 2026 at the crossroads of Armenian warmth and French baking traditions.

Soleil Bakery was created by master baker Meri, who spent years training with top pastry chefs in Paris before returning home to bring that expertise to Yerevan. Every croissant, every loaf, every tart carries the soul of two cultures.
